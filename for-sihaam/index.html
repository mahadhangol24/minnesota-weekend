01 / The letter

My sincerest apology

To the one who brings me joy, understands me, and grants the wishes I ask of her—the one and only Sihaam. This letter is for you, and I offer you my sincerest apology. I pray it will be my last, because I truly want to make things right. I’ll do what it takes, but I also want you to understand where I’m coming from and how afraid I am of losing you again.

I know we love each other deeply, and that we've said or done things that have hurt one another or brought tears to our eyes. I don’t want that for us anymore. Sihaam, I know what it takes to make you feel happy, comfortable, loved, and protected. I also know what I want from you, and it’s simple: a happy, peaceful relationship where we understand each other.

You are my girl, Sihaam. Starting today, and hopefully moving forward, I want us to have a clear understanding. I want you to listen to me and let me lead us, because I have a plan that I believe will work in our favor, as long as Allah SWT wills it. I want you to know that I love and care for you immensely, and I’ll always show you that and provide for you so you never have to worry about a thing.

All I ask in return is respect, that you listen attentively to what I have to say, that we don't look at each other indifferently when we disagree, and that we both try to be understanding and compassionate. I want us both to feel optimistic and excited about us and our future.

I needed to step away and process everything this weekend. My frustration wasn’t mainly about you, Sihaam. I’ve been dealing with a lot, and it took a toll on me. I let my stress spill over onto the people who matter most to me, as well as other external factors, and that wasn’t fair at all. You deserve the best of me, and I’ll give you that going forward, as I always have.

I also want you to know that I’ll be in SoCal this weekend to see you and view one of the offices at Culver city. I’d love for us to have a beautiful dinner in Hollywood at Casa Madera or Geoffrey’s, if it's open. I really want to see you and spend quality time together, strengthening our relationship and treating you the way your man, Mahad, always does.

Please, Sihaam, I pray you’ll accept my apology and allow us to move forward in the best possible way.
